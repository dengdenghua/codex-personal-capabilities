# Sources used to build this skill

## Open-source template

- [123ww-wq/SW_GB_Drawing_Templates](https://github.com/123ww-wq/SW_GB_Drawing_Templates) — SolidWorks 2023 GB drawing templates for A0/A3/A4 and sheet formats. MIT licensed.
- Downloaded into `templates/` for local reuse.

## Standards referenced

- `GB/T 14689` — technical drawing: sheet sizes and layout.
- `GB/T 10609.1` — technical drawing: title blocks.
- `GB/T 4458.4` — mechanical drawings: dimensioning.
- `GB/T 1804` — general tolerances.
- `GB/T 1182` — geometrical tolerancing.
- `ISO 128` — technical drawings: general principles of presentation.
- `ISO 7200` — title blocks.
- `ISO 2768-1` — general tolerances.
- `ISO 1101` — geometrical tolerancing.
- `ASME Y14.100` — engineering drawing practices.
- `ASME Y14.3` — orthographic and pictorial views.
- `ASME Y14.5` — dimensioning and tolerancing.

> Standard clauses are not copied here. Check the official or authorized copy when a claim must be formally compliant.
